H. Peter Hofstee is a distinguished research staff member at IBM, working in infrastructure development in Austin, TX. Peter has worked at IBM since 1996 and has been associated with power architecture processors and systems for his entire career working in both research and development. He contributed to the first 1GHz CMOS microprocessor, the Cell Broadband Engine processor, used in systems ranging from the Sony PlayStation 3 to the world's first 1 Petaflop supercomputer. Peter is also an active participant in efforts to create new open source and free architecture-compliant cores.  Peter received his education in physics at Groningen University in the Netherlands, and in Computer Science at Caltech where he also taught VLSI and computer science classes prior to joining IBM. Peter has remained active in academia throughout his career, most recently as a part-time professor (now emeritus) at Delft Technical University in the Netherlands.
